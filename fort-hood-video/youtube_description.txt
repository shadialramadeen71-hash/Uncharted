TITLE:
The Fort Hood Shooting: How Nidal Hasan Was Stopped — and Why His Case Is Back in the News

DESCRIPTION:
On November 5, 2009, U.S. Army Major Nidal Malik Hasan, an Army psychiatrist, opened fire inside the Soldier Readiness Processing Center at Fort Hood, Texas. In about ten minutes, 13 people were killed and more than 30 were wounded. This documentary covers his background, the warning signs, the attack and the people who fought back. It also explains how base police officers Kimberly Munley and Mark Todd stopped him, plus his court-martial, his death sentence and the execution the Army has scheduled for December 3, 2026.
