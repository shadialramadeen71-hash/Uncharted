TITLE:
The Hunt for Bin Laden: How SEAL Team Six Found the World's Most Wanted Man

DESCRIPTION:
From the caves of Tora Bora to a walled compound in Abbottabad: the ten-year hunt for Osama bin Laden, explained by Professor Hoot.

Did Delta Force get bin Laden? Not quite. Delta Force came close at Tora Bora in 2001, but it was SEAL Team Six (DEVGRU) that carried out Operation Neptune Spear on May 2, 2011.
